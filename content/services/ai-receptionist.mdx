## Eliminate Missed Opportunities at the Front Door

Every unanswered inbound call represents lost revenue and degraded client trust. Standard voicemail trees and offshore answering desks frustrate callers with long delays and rigid menus.

The TechCentera AI Receptionist delivers human-grade conversation with sub-second response times. It understands complex operational context, schedules consultations directly onto executive calendars, and updates your records in real time.

## Governed Voice Architecture Built for Enterprise Reliability

Unlike generic voice wrappers, our receptionist systems run on governed decision boundaries. Every interaction is constrained to verified enterprise data, preventing hallucinations while ensuring accurate intake.

Your team receives immediate notification summaries with transcriptions and sentiment tags, enabling rapid follow-up on high-priority inquiries.
