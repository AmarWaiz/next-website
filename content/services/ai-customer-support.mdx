## Support Velocity Without the Overhead Scaling Trap

As transaction volume expands, support teams face spiraling costs and escalating response times. Offshore teams require constant oversight, while traditional keyword bots irritate customers.

TechCentera builds dedicated AI customer support engines that understand domain nuances, resolve transactional workflows end-to-end, and preserve brand authority.

## Transparent Triage with Full Operator Control

Your human specialists never lose visibility. When complex inquiries arise, the AI compiles a concise briefing package with source citations, customer history, and proposed resolutions.

The operator simply reviews and approves, slashing handle times from 15 minutes to 30 seconds.
