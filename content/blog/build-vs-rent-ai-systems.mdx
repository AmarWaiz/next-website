# Build vs. Rent: Why Owning Your AI Infrastructure Beats Multi-Tenant SaaS

When companies begin exploring AI automation, the easiest path appears to be subscribing to turnkey SaaS platforms. These tools promise instant setup with low initial friction.

However, as usage expands across departments, the hidden costs of renting software become painfully apparent: escalating per-seat licenses, severe rate limits, and zero visibility into how proprietary data is processed.

## The Multi-Tenant SaaS Tax

SaaS vendors price their tools to extract maximum value as your company scales. A team growing from 20 to 100 operators often sees annual software bills quadruple without receiving any additional core capability.

Furthermore, multi-tenant architectures force your workflows into rigid molds. When your operational advantage comes from proprietary fulfillment processes or custom pricing models, SaaS tools cannot adapt.

## The Total Cost of Ownership Comparison

When you commission bespoke AI infrastructure, the economics fundamentally shift:

| Metric | Multi-Tenant SaaS | Custom Owned Infrastructure |
|---|---|---|
| **License Fees** | $150–$300 / user / month | $0 recurring licensing fees |
| **Data Privacy** | Shared cloud / Vendor terms | 100% private in your VPC |
| **Workflow Flexibility** | Restricted to vendor APIs | Tailored to your exact data model |
| **3-Year TCO** | Scales linearly with headcount | Fixed investment with marginal compute |

## Retaining Intellectual Property

Software that runs your core business operations is an enterprise asset. By owning the full codebase, database schemas, and AI orchestration pipelines, you turn operational efficiency into lasting enterprise valuation.
